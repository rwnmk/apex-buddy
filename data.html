Team Comp Data

Every rating the Team Comp Analyser uses, straight from the files in data/. Skim the charts, and if something looks wrong, say so: that's the point of this page.

What the score means: how well these players can carry out the comp's plan, and how well the comp covers what the game throws at you, given no comp covers everything. It's tuned for ranked. A plan's "punishes" and "risks" are advice: they aren't scored, because the tool can't know what's in your lobby.
