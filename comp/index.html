Team Comp Analyser

Input or paste in your group's picks, tick who's playing, then select a map and finetune by the traits you're after. Pick from the generated comps to review the breakdown, notes and perk suggestions.

The score is how well your players can carry out the comp's plan and cover what the game throws at you. Tuned for ranked; a plan's "punishes" and "risks" are advice, not scored. (No refunds can be provided for RP lost as a result of trusting the output.)
